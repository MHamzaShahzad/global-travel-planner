# Sources for Verification

## Visa and entry (official first)
- The destination's own immigration or foreign-ministry eVisa portal (always the final authority)
- IATA Travel Centre / Timatic (used by airlines for boarding decisions)
- Schengen: the consulate of the main-destination country; home-affairs.ec.europa.eu; travel-europe.europa.eu (ETIAS/EES)
- UK: gov.uk/check-uk-visa; UK ETA
- US: travel.state.gov; ESTA (official site only: esta.cbp.dhs.gov)
- Canada: canada.ca (eTA / visitor visa)
- Australia: immi.homeaffairs.gov.au
- UAE (for residents travelling out or visitors coming in): icp.gov.ae; gdrfad.gov.ae
- Saudi Arabia (tourism, Umrah): visa.mofa.gov.sa; Nusuk

## Travel advisories
- UK FCDO (gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice), US State Department, Australia Smartraveller, Canada travel.gc.ca

## Logistics
- Airline and airport official sites; Google Flights/Skyscanner for route discovery (verify on the airline site)
- National rail operators (e.g. SNCF, Trenitalia, DB, Renfe, JR, SBB)
- Official attraction websites for hours, closures, and timed-entry booking
- Official tourism boards for events and holidays

Do not rely on forums, social media, or unofficial "visa service" sites for rules. Flag unofficial ESTA/ETA lookalike sites, which charge inflated fees.
