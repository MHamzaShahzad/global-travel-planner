# Full Trip Plan Template

## 1. Trip summary
Destination(s), dates, duration, travellers, style, total budget. One or two lines on why this plan fits these travellers.

## 2. Recommended route
City/country sequence with nights per base, e.g. Istanbul (4) → Cappadocia (2) → Antalya (3). Note open-jaw flights if used.

## 3. Day-by-day itinerary
Per day: base, morning / afternoon / evening, transport between stops, and booking-ahead flags. Travel days are shown as travel days.

## 4. Accommodation strategy
Recommended area per base (with the reason), category, and booking timing. Specific properties only if verified.

## 5. Transportation plan
International flights (likely routings, direct vs connecting), intercity legs (mode, duration, booking tips), local transport, and airport transfers.

## 6. Estimated budget
| Category | Range (per person / total) | Notes |
Flights, accommodation, transport, food, attractions/activities, visa fees, insurance, SIM/eSIM, emergency buffer (~10%). Ranges, not false precision; mark what was verified vs estimated.

## 7. Visa & entry requirements
Per traveller nationality and per country, including transits: visa type, fee, processing time, documents, passport validity, and ETA-type authorisations. Include the date verified and a reminder to reconfirm.

## 8. Travel tips
Customs and etiquette, dress codes, payments and tipping, SIM/eSIM, safety and scams, weather and packing, food notes (halal/vegetarian), and useful apps.

## 9. Alternatives
Cheaper, faster, more luxurious, and less crowded variants, one or two lines each.
