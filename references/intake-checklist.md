# Traveller Intake Checklist

Extract these from the conversation first. Ask only about gaps that change the plan.

## Travellers
- Passport nationality (each traveller, as nationalities may differ within a group)
- Country of residence and residence status (e.g. UAE/GCC residence visa); other valid visas held (US, UK, Schengen, Canada, Japan, etc.)
- Number of travellers, ages (children's exact ages matter for pricing and pace), mobility or accessibility needs
- Previous travel experience, and destinations already visited

## Trip
- Departure city or airport
- Dates, or how flexible they are; trip length; leave constraints
- Purpose: honeymoon, family, adventure, culture, religious, shopping, food, business/bleisure, etc.
- Single-destination vs multi-country; willingness to change hotels often

## Preferences
- Budget (total or per person; what it includes) and travel style (backpacker, mid-range, comfort, luxury)
- Accommodation: hotel, boutique, apartment, resort, hostel; preferred star level; room configuration
- Transport: flight class, comfort with trains, buses, or self-driving
- Food: halal, vegetarian, allergies, adventurousness
- Activities: pace, interests, must-dos, things to avoid
- Religious or cultural considerations (prayer times, Friday prayers, Ramadan timing)

## Practical
- Passport validity (usually 6+ months beyond return) and blank pages
- Travel insurance status
- Payment methods (international cards, cash needs)
- Connectivity (eSIM vs roaming)
